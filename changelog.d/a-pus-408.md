### Features

- The archive can now live in a Team folder, or in an external storage that is mounted for several users or groups, such as the media folder of Paperless mounted read-only for a group. Such a mount has no account of its own that owns its files, so until now its documents never showed in the search. Name it with `occ paperless_unified_search:trusted-mounts team:3 external:7`, a Team folder by its ID and an external storage by its mount ID, and each user who may only read a file there finds its document in the search, on the dashboard and in notifications. A user who may write there sees none of its documents, since they could have named a file after any document: *Try a search* and `occ paperless_unified_search:search --explain` say so, the settings list the trusted mounts below the shares, and `occ paperless_unified_search:check` says that it can't count their files. Trust only a mount that nobody but the synchronization writes; see decision 0019.
