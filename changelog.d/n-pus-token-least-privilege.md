### Features

- **A warning about a token that may do too much, or too little:** most people paste the API token of their own Paperless account, a superuser, and the test of the connection said that it succeeded; whoever got hold of that token could change and delete every document. Now **Test connection and save**, `occ paperless_unified_search:setup`, `test` and `check` ask Paperless what the account of the token may do, and the settings show a warning below the token, `occ` a line and the administration overview a warning, with what to change, when the account is a superuser or an admin of Paperless or may add, change or delete documents. They also warn when it may not view correspondents or document types, whose names the results then lack. The token is still saved, and the search works as before. Paperless tells this only to an account that may view its UI settings, so give the Paperless account of the search **View** for **Documents**, **Correspondents**, **Document Types** and **UI Settings**, as the documentation now says.
