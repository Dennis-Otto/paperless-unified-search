### Bug fixes

- A user who can open only a small part of a large archive, such as their own documents or a shared folder, now finds their documents for a common word such as *invoice* even when more than 100 documents of others rank in front of them: while a search has found no result yet, it goes through up to 500 hits of Paperless instead of 100, within the same 3 seconds. The FAQ explains what a search does when even more documents of others rank first.
