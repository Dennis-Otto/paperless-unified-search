### Bug fixes

- **Only the archive shared read-only counts:** a file of the archive account stands for a document only for a user who may only read it. Before, whoever could write into a folder of the archive account, through a share with write permission, could give a file there the marker of any document, such as `Copy [P999].pdf`, and see the title and the excerpt of document 999. If Paperless documents disappear from your search after the update, the archive account shares their folder with you with write permission: ask your administrator to share it read-only. `occ paperless_unified_search:search --explain` and **Try a search** call such a document *writable*.
- **See how the archive is shared:** the settings show below the archive account with how many users, groups and teams it shares its files and how many public links it has, such as *Shared read-only: 1 user, 2 groups.*, and warn about each share that allows changes, with its path and its recipient. `occ paperless_unified_search:check` lists the same, and the overview of the administration settings warns until every share is read-only.
