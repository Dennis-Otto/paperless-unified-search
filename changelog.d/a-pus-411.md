### Features

- **Find similar documents in Paperless** in the menu of a file in Files whose name carries the marker of a document, such as `[P412]`: it shows the documents that Paperless finds similar to that one, as the search term `like:412` finds them, in a dialog with the preview and the title of each, and a click opens the file. No term to type, and no need to turn on **Search connected services** first.
