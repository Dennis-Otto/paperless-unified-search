### Bug fixes

- **The Traditional Chinese listing in the App Store uses the words of Nextcloud for Taiwan:** it called the credentials manager of Nextcloud, which keeps the API token, 認證資訊管理器, and now 憑證管理器, as Nextcloud and Paperless Sync name it, and a client of the search API 客戶端 instead of 用戶端, as Nextcloud writes it. The translation check now finds such a slip by itself, as it does for Paperless Sync: a word of Simplified Chinese in the Traditional Chinese texts, such as 日誌 or 用戶, with the word of Taiwan, and a dash written as one — or with spaces in either Chinese.
