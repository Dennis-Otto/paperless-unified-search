### Features

- **Find the documents similar to one:** Paperless's *More like this*, which finds the other letters of a case, now works from Nextcloud. Search for `like:[P412]`, with the marker that you see in the file name, or for `like:P412` or `like:412`, in the global search, in the smart picker or through the API: the results are the documents most similar to that one whose files you can open, without the document itself, with the date filter and every page of results. An ID that Paperless doesn't know, or of a document that its account may not view, finds nothing and is no problem of the search; *Try a search* in the settings and `occ paperless_unified_search:search` say so. Before Paperless-ngx 2.20.13, Paperless doesn't check whether its account may view that document, so one that it may not view still finds similar documents, of those that its account may view.
- **More fields of Paperless's search, documented:** the table under *Search like in Paperless* now also shows `title:`, `content:`, `asn:`, `notes:`, `path:`, `custom_fields:` and `original_filename:`, and says that `owner:` works in Paperless-ngx 2 alone. The contract tests check every row against real Paperless-ngx 2 and 3.
