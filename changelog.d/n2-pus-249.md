### Features

- **Tags in the results and on the dashboard:** most Paperless archives are sorted by their tags, such as *unpaid*, *tax 2026* or *warranty*, but a result showed only the date, the correspondent and the type, so two invoices of the same correspondent looked the same although one was *paid* and the other *unpaid*. Now each result of the search and each document of *New in Paperless* on the dashboard shows up to two of its tags after its type, then how many more it has, such as *Aug 12, 2026 · Northstar Energy · Bill · unpaid · tax 2026 · +1*. The tag of the inbox, which Paperless gives every new document, never shows. One request names the tags of a whole page, and Nextcloud keeps their names for ten minutes, as it does for correspondents and types, and *Try a search* in the settings lists the tags of each document too. Give the Paperless account of the search **View** for **Tag** as well: until then the results show without tags, as before. `occ paperless_unified_search:check` warns at once that the account may not view tags, and the settings and the administration overview warn from the next test of the connection, so test it again after the update.
