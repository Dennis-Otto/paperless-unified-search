### Bug fixes

- **A warning when results lack correspondents or types:** Paperless applies the permissions of each object to correspondents and types too, so an account that may view them still sees no name of one that another user owns, such as an administrator who added it in the web interface. Results and the dashboard then showed no correspondent and no type, without a word. The test of the connection now compares what the 25 newest documents name with what the account can see, and warns in the settings, in `occ` and in the overview, with what to change.
- **Pictures of the Paperless side of the set-up:** the guide and the READMEs now show the dialogs of the account, the workflow, the permissions of correspondents and types and the API token, each light and dark.
