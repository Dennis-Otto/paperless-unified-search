### Bug fixes

- **A warning when results lack correspondents, types or tags:** Paperless applies the permissions of each object to correspondents, types and tags too, so an account that may view them still sees no name of one that another user owns, such as an administrator who added it in the web interface. Results and the dashboard then showed no correspondent, no type and no tag, without a word. The test of the connection now compares what the 25 newest documents name with what the account can see, and warns in the settings, in `occ` and in the overview, with what to change.
- **Pictures of the Paperless side of the set-up:** the guide now shows the dialogs of the account, the workflow, the permissions of correspondents and types and the API token, each light and dark, and the READMEs the dialog of the account.
