### Features

- While Paperless is down, all of Nextcloud stays responsive: after a search request that gets no answer at all, because Paperless doesn't answer in time, refuses the connection or its name doesn't resolve, the searches of the next 30 seconds, of every user, show no Paperless documents without asking Paperless, instead of each holding a PHP worker of Nextcloud for up to 10 seconds per keystroke. This matters most with *Always include Paperless in global search* or the personal switch on, where every search of Nextcloud asks Paperless. An answer of Paperless with an error starts no rest, the failure is in *Search problems* as before, and **Test connection and save** or `occ paperless_unified_search:test` ends the rest at once when Paperless answers the test. Without a distributed memory cache, every search asks Paperless, as before.
