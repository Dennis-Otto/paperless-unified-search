### Features

- **Search problems** in the administration settings now says in the column **Error** what failed and what to do, in the language of the administrator and in the words of the test of the connection, such as *Paperless did not answer in time*, for a request to Paperless that failed or that only its second try answered. The kind and the message of the error stay below it, as they are, for a bug report. Problems recorded before the update, and those of finding the files in Nextcloud, show the error alone, as before.
- A user whom Paperless Sync lets see only the documents with some tags now reads which tags those are at the top of the section **Paperless** of their personal settings, such as *Your administrator lets you find only the documents that have one of these tags in Paperless: Katja, Household.*, so that a search that comes back empty for another document no longer looks broken. The section shows for such a user even when the administrators let users choose neither of its settings.
