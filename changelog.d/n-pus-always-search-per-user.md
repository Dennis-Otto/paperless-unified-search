### Features

- **Always search your own Paperless documents:** Nextcloud switches *Search connected services* off on every page that loads, so Paperless results disappeared until you found the switch again. Now each user can select **Always search my Paperless documents** under *Personal settings → Paperless*, and every search of their own includes Paperless from the next page on, without the switch; nobody else’s searches change. Administrators no longer have to choose between sending every term of every user to Paperless and asking everyone to turn the switch on again: they keep *Always include Paperless in global search* for everybody, or turn off the new **Let users include Paperless in all their searches** (on by default, also `occ paperless_unified_search:setup --no-personal-always-search`), which hides the choice and ignores what users chose.
