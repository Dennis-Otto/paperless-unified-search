# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Everybody who takes part in issues, discussions, pull requests and reviews agrees to it.

## Our standards

Participation in this project must be respectful, constructive and welcoming, regardless of experience, nationality, age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, religion or any other personal characteristic. Examples of positive behavior:

- giving actionable feedback and accepting it gracefully;
- accepting technical disagreement professionally and assuming good intent;
- respecting privacy, in particular around the issues, logs and repositories of others;
- focusing discussions on the project and its users.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized language or imagery, doxxing, deliberate disruption and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report behavior that violates this code privately to the maintainer:

- use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, issue or discussion; or
- open a private report through the channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and start its title with "Conduct:".

Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer acknowledges a report within 7 days.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Depending on the severity, responses range from a private correction and a public warning to a temporary or permanent ban. Enforcement decisions are proportionate, documented when appropriate, and protect the privacy and safety of affected people.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project in public spaces.
