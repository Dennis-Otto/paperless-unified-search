# Code of conduct

## Our standards

Participation in this project must be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Examples of positive behavior include giving actionable feedback, accepting technical disagreement professionally, respecting privacy, and focusing discussions on the project and its users.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Public conduct concerns can be reported to the maintainer through a GitHub issue when appropriate. Sensitive reports should use the private security-reporting channel documented in `SECURITY.md`. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented when appropriate, and protect the privacy and safety of affected people.
